2. 제3자 제공과 처리 위탁

  • aitestbed.kr AI 게이트웨이(국내, 모두의 AI 실험실 지원 플랫폼): 통해 서버가 AI 모델을 부를 때 거치는 중계.
  • Microsoft Azure Translator(Microsoft, 한국 중부 지역 리소스): 번역.
  • Anthropic(미국): 다음 할 말 추천과 확인할 내용 판별.
  • MyMemory(Translated, 이탈리아): 서버를 쓸 수 없을 때의 대체 번역.
  • Apple·Google: 휴대폰 음성 인식과 음성 출력. 각 회사의 개인정보 정책을 따릅니다.

위의 경우 말고는 정보를 다른 곳에 제공하거나 판매하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy (English)

Tonghae has no sign-up and does not collect names, phone numbers, or location.

  • Voice: processed only while the mic button is on, by your phone's speech recognition (Apple or Google). Tonghae's server never receives audio.
  • Conversation text: after you agree to sending text to external AI, it goes to the Tonghae server for translation, suggested replies, and amount checks. The server sends it to Microsoft Azure Translator for translation, and through the aitestbed.kr AI gateway to an AI model (Anthropic Claude) for suggested replies and checks. If the server is unavailable, the app sends text directly to the MyMemory translation service. The server does not store conversation content.
  • Saved records (date, checked items, amounts from the conversation) stay on your device only, up to 30.
  • Usage logs: time, situation type, language, response time, and token counts only. No conversation content or IP addresses. Deleted after the project ends.
  • Settings (language, speed, volume, voice) stay on your device.

Please do not say or type sensitive data such as ID or account passwords. Contact: 1124394@naver.com
